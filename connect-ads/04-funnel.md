## Short explainer (the “VSL,” cut down for $99)

Sixty to ninety seconds, on-page, optional on mute-friendly captions. Same belief as the ads. Not a 20-minute pitch.

**On-screen title:** Another tab won’t remember.

**Spoken outline:**

1. **0–10s, problem.** You already have a chatbot. You already paid for a plan. It still starts from zero tomorrow.
2. **10–25s, amplify.** So you open a second tab. Then a third. None of them hold the work, and none of them wait for you before they can send, spend, or post — unless you build that rule yourself.
3. **25–50s, mechanism.** A desk is named. It keeps continuity. It drafts. You click yes on send, spend, and public. That’s the difference, not a prettier chat window.
4. **50–75s, offer.** I wire the path you pick — Hermes, Muse, Grok Bot, OpenClaw, a vendor assistant, or one of the others on the page — for $99 once. Start-package connections are included. Later wires are $1. You still pay the vendor their plan.
5. **75–90s, response.** Pick a path. Email jpanasuk@gmail.com. If you want help choosing, take the quiz first.

No testimonial reel until real customers have given something you can show.
