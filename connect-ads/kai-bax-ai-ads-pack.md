## The public formula, step by step

### A. Write the offer before you write the ad

From the March 29, 2022 thread ([start](https://x.com/itskaibax/status/1508803544220332032)):

1. What goals and pains does the audience actually have?
2. How is this better than 5–10 competing offers (service, promise, guarantee, bonuses)?
3. How do you make the buyer’s life easier?
4. Can you splinter a cheaper entry-point offer from the main offer?
5. Can you reverse risk or add a bold guarantee — after looking at the offer like a skeptic?
6. Does the copy name the *transformation*, not just the product?
7. What real proof can you show? Ask raving customers for a review or a video. Consider a referral reward.
8. Is there honest scarcity or limited capacity?
9. Is the offer worth about 10x the price to the customer (he cites Russell Brunson’s check)?

He also posted, separately, that you should try collecting more cash up front (for example, presenting a 3-month total instead of a monthly price, then downselling a payment plan if needed). That tactic is for high-ticket retainers. It does not apply to a locked $99 CONNECT setup.

### B. The ad itself: three blocks, in this order

From the Oct 25, 2022 thread “simple ad formula” ([start](https://x.com/itskaibax/status/1584743850349846529)). He claimed that version of the formula booked 1,347 sales calls in 28 days — his claim, not a house number.

**1. Offer (first three lines)**

Framework, in his words: *MARKET CALL OUT* (to qualify who should stop scrolling) + *we will INSERT PRE-EXISTING DESIRE OF YOUR MARKET or GUARANTEE*.

The call-out is a filter, not a flourish. “AI Automation Agencies:” at the top of his later Meta ads is the same move.

**2. Proof, immediately after the offer**

He says every claim needs three pieces of evidence, and that results/track record — not a bigger promise — is how you look superior. “Anyone can make a bold claim.” Put proof right after the offer, not at the end.

**3. Mechanism**

The “how.” Its job is to install one belief: you are the best or only option for the outcome they already want. Do that by explaining, at the same time, why what they already tried failed *and* why this is different. After they hear the mechanism, they should be able to say: “That’s why the last thing didn’t work, and why this would.”

He also says you only compete in three places: offer, results/track record, and solution/mechanism ([Oct 27, 2022](https://x.com/itskaibax/status/1585468623312883718)). The offer gets attention. Proof creates superiority. The mechanism installs the belief that turns a click into a buyer.

Public Meta ads from ScaleClients (2025–2026) still follow that skeleton, then add a numbered “here’s what I’ll do,” named client anecdotes, and a single CTA: book a call. Common headlines in ad-library scrapes: “Use This Acquisition AI For $0 Down,” “Pay me after this AI gets you clients,” “More sales calls (guaranteed).” Those are *his* offer promises to agencies. Do not transplant them onto CONNECT.

### C. The four-step funnel behind the ad

From the Oct 26, 2022 thread ([start](https://x.com/itskaibax/status/1585361424175534095)). He claimed he had scaled his agency and “dozens of clients” to $100k/month+ with this funnel — again, his claim.

1. **Opt-in page.** Tie the thing the market wants to the data you need. His example: if you want a real phone number, tell them you’ll text the offer information to that number. High opt-in rate *and* clean data.
2. **VSL page.** Install the same single belief: this is the best or only option. Same dual mechanism as the ad (why past attempts failed + why this is different).
3. **Book-a-call page.** Calendar sits immediately after the VSL. Ask enough questions to know whether to cancel a bad-fit call, but not so many that you add friction.
4. **Thank-you page.** You already sold the booking. Now sell the *show-up*. Paid traffic will arrive asking “is this person legit?” Answer that in a thank-you video.

**Show-up bonus (public):** call every booked appointment before the scheduled time. He said that move took his show-up rate to 92% on a $9,800 offer ([tweet](https://x.com/itskaibax/status/1585361440512380929)).

**Later productization (ScaleClients site + reviews, 2025–2026):**

- Run the ads on Meta, mostly video, mostly US reach.
- Qualify before the calendar (the site currently claims a 92% “filter rate” — their metric, undefined in public copy).
- Automate confirmations, reminders, and no-show rebooking in GoHighLevel. The sales page markets a “Fast Five” email series (they claim it produces half their appointments) and a no-show automation (they claim 76% rebook, 98% of those show). Those figures are sales-page claims, not audited results.
- Close with a “Reverse Sales Playbook” (not published).
- Send *qualified* close/booking data back to Meta so the algorithm hunts more of the people who actually buy, not just people who click. This is the one media-buying idea that transfers cleanly to any offer, including $99.

Tools named in public material: Meta Ads, GoHighLevel, AI for image-ad prompts and UGC scripts, a “UGC creator rolodex” for paid students, email and SMS. The “hidden ad audience” is advertised as a secret. It is not disclosed in any free source found this turn.

### D. What his ads actually sound like (structure only)

A typical 2026 ScaleClients primary text, summarized from a public ad-library capture:

- Niche call-out in the first line
- A done-for-you promise of daily qualified calls, plus “pay after it gets you results”
- A personal scale story
- Three numbered deliverables (ads, close-rate coaching, an “operating system”)
- A stack of named-client cash-collected anecdotes
- One CTA: click and book a call

That is useful as a *shape*. The numbers and the $0-down guarantee are his commercial offer, not a template for CONNECT.
