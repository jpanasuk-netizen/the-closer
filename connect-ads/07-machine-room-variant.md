**Status:** draft only. No uploads, no end-screen changes, no paid promotion until Jeremy gates them.

Kai Bax’s formula is a **high-ticket appointment funnel** for agencies and coaches. Machine Room and Ret Paladin are **YouTube channels**, not $3k retainers. The ad *shape* still helps: call-out, hook, mechanism, proof you actually have, one CTA. The close-on-a-call machine does not fit, and CONNECT should not be hard-sold to a gaming or documentary audience unless Jeremy explicitly wants that crossover.

Public surfaces to keep straight:

- Machine Room is the finance / documentary channel (house long-form such as the SVB piece). GitHub/profile references use @themachineroomtoday.
- Ret Paladin / “Big J Ret” is the WoW channel, handle @3jsa7.

If a URL or handle has drifted, check the channel page before publishing and put the real one in the end screen. Do not invent subscriber counts, watch hours, or RPM.

## Where the formula fits

| Bax public step | YouTube version |
| --- | --- |
| Market call-out | First line of the title, hook, or pinned comment names the watcher (“If you traded through 2023 and still don’t know who actually ate the SVB loss…” / “If you main ret and your seals still drift…”) |
| Desire or truthful offer | The video will show the mechanism of the story or the rotation — not a lead guarantee |
| Proof immediately after | Real footage, documents, patch numbers, a vod timestamp, a parse. No fake “banks paid me $X” or “this guide 2x’d my rating” unless that’s a house receipt |
| Mechanism | Why the usual explanation is wrong, and what actually happened / how the rotation actually works |
| CTA | One: watch the long, subscribe, or grab a listed free brief. Soft. No 45-minute close. |
| Thank-you / follow-up | End screen + pinned comment + optional email if a voluntary list exists. Don’t build a GHL appointment funnel for this. |

## Machine Room — organic Short / bumper scripts

These are content, not CONNECT ads. If a Short is ever used as a paid hook, it should still land on the long video, not on a checkout.

### MR-1 — Mechanism hook (~20s)

**Muted first-frame:** The usual story is wrong

**Spoken (template — fill the blanks with facts from the actual episode, never placeholders in a published cut):**

> If you followed [event] in the headlines, you were told [common story]. That’s the easy version. The receipts go the other way: [one concrete mechanism]. The long video walks the documents. Link’s on the channel.

**CTA:** Watch the long. No price. No “book a call.”

**Need before this can ship:** the specific event, the false story, and one cited receipt from that video. Leave [NEED RECEIPT] rather than inventing them.

### MR-2 — After-state hook (~15s)

**Muted first-frame:** Watch this before the next one

**Spoken (template):**

> Before the next [bank / fund / blow-up] cycle gets a cartoon explanation, here’s how the last one actually cleared. Full walkthrough on Machine Room. Sound on for the documents, or read the captions.

Same rule: no invented document names.

### Machine Room static / title lines (test, don’t invent the topic)

- The usual story is wrong
- Follow the receipts
- Here’s what the headline skipped
- Watch the long, not the recap

**Pinned comment draft (after a long goes up):**

> If you want the full walk, it’s this video, not the Short. I don’t sell a trading signal off the documentary. Questions about the documents, leave them here.

Do not bridge this into CONNECT in the first pinned comment. A quiet mention belongs, if anywhere, on the house hub or a community post Jeremy approves: people who want a desk can see CONNECT. People who came for the film should get the film.

**Muted first-frame:** Your seals are drifting
