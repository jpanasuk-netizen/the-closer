## What ENTERPRISE is (locked facts only)

- It wires an entire enterprise's agent paths into one working setup — the same desk idea as CONNECT, at company scale.
- The human gate stays: nothing sends, spends, or posts publicly without a human yes. At enterprise scale that gate is an org rule, not just one person's habit.
- Vendor plans are still a separate bill — same as personal. ENTERPRISE is the integration, not the vendors' plans.
- Price starts at $10,000 and is set by scope. There is no fixed ceiling stated anywhere yet.

## Enterprise funnel notes (draft)

- Opt-in: a one-page enterprise brief (what "integrate your entire enterprise" means once Jeremy defines scope) — not the personal quiz. The personal quiz answers "which path"; the enterprise brief answers "how many seats, which systems."
- VSL: one belief — "your company's agent work should remember yesterday." 60–90 seconds, same shape as the personal explainer, different buyer.
- Calendar: this is the tier where a real qualification call belongs. Light qualification only: company, seat count, which agent plans they already pay for, who decides. No 10-question application.
- Thank-you page: sells the show-up — what to bring to the call (seat count, current tools), who from their side should attend, and that the $10,000+ is scope-priced, not a flat quote in the email.
- Follow-up: one email, next business day — "here's the brief, here's the call time, reply with seat count if you haven't." No Fast Five sequence until a real close rate exists to learn from.
