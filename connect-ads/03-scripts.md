## Script 1 — “I already had the chatbot”

**Audience:** People who already pay for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok  
**Awareness:** Solution-aware, product-unaware  
**Length:** ~22 seconds

**Muted first-frame:** I already had the chatbot

**On-screen by beat:**
- 0–3s: I already had the chatbot
- 3–8s: It forgot yesterday
- 8–15s: You still click yes
- 15–22s: $99 to wire it up

**Spoken:**

> I already had the chatbot. I paid for the plan. And every morning it still didn’t know what I told it last week. I’m not buying another tab. I want a desk with a name, drafts I can check, and a rule that nothing goes out — mail, money, or a public post — until I click yes. That’s CONNECT. $99 to wire the path you already picked into your life. Future wires are $1. You pay the vendor for their plan. You keep the gate. If that’s what you wanted, email jpanasuk@gmail.com and name the path.

**Peacock:** Phone-shot at a real desk, chat app visible with no private text readable. Not a studio.

| PASTOR | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Problem | Chatbot already paid for, still no continuity |
| Amplify | Starts from zero every morning |
| Story / solution | Named desk, drafts, gate |
| Transformation | Work carries over; nothing leaves without a yes |
| Offer | $99 wire, $1 later, vendor plan separate |
| Response | Email jpanasuk@gmail.com, name the path |

**NESB:** New (desk vs tab), Easy (email a path, $99 once), Safe (you keep the gate; no fake guarantee), Big (continuity + $1 later wires). Stacks 4.

**Objections killed:** “I already have ChatGPT.” “Will it send or spend on its own?” “Is $99 a subscription?”

**Slop pass:** Y. Cut “unlock,” “game-changer,” any invented “hours saved.”

## Quick test matrix (copy only)

Run one body (A, B, C, D, or E) against three first-frame lines before you touch targeting. Kill the hook that can’t be understood with the sound off. See [06-targeting-testing.md](06-targeting-testing.md).

Do not add “guaranteed appointments,” “pay after results,” or any ScaleClients proof to these variants.
