How to read the matrix

The six certs you already have

Twenty years of delivery is the credential: production competence, warehouse/domain depth, systems thinking, communication, leadership and customer fit. Do not dilute those with “I write clean code.” Name the floor and the receipt.

The one new layer

Agentic AI is the lab year. Not a certificate: a stood-up path from prompt to tool to model and back, with MCP, contracts, telemetry, gates and packets someone else can inspect.

The three-day stand-up

Day 1 chat path. Day 2 coding pack. Day 3 memory and workflow. The next 18 days are repetition until you can teach the stack without notes, with qwen still up.

The canonical journey

From Informatica recovery → private RTX lab → UNIFIED → MCP → packets → hunt board. The portfolio is a trail of operational evidence, not a mood board.
