GET IT OUT THERE — 8 minutes

Folder: POST_THESE
Do these in order. Don't overthink it.

1) COVER PHOTO (90 seconds)
   LinkedIn → your profile → camera on the banner → Upload photo
   Pick: 1-linkedin-cover-banner.png
   Position it, Save.

2) PROFILE PHOTO
   Click your round photo → Update photo
   Pick: 2-profile-photo.jpg

3) HEADLINE
   Pencil next to your name
   Paste HEADLINE.txt

4) THE POST (this is the one that travels)
   Home → Start a post
   Paste LINKEDIN_POST.txt
   Click the video icon → upload 4-feed-video.mp4
   ALSO attach 3-feed-poster.png if LinkedIn lets you do both
   Post as Public.

5) FEATURED (stays on your profile)
   Profile → Featured → Add → Add a photo
   Upload 3-feed-poster.png
   After the site is live, Featured → Add a link → paste the GitHub Pages URL.

6) OPEN TO WORK
   Profile → Open to → Finding a new job
   Titles: AI Systems Engineer, AI Engineer, Data Engineer, Integration Architect
   Locations: United States · Remote
   Visible to: All LinkedIn members

HOST THE SITE (optional, 3 minutes)
   Right-click PUBLISH-GITHUB-PAGES.ps1 in the parent folder → Run with PowerShell
   Log into GitHub in the browser when it asks.
   URL will be: https://jpanasuk-netizen.github.io/the-closer/
   Put that URL in Featured, your About, and the post comments.
