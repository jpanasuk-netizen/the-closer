What does $99.99 include?

$99.99 is the one-time setup to wire your chosen path into your life, including the connections in the start package. Future connections are $1 each. It does not include the monthly plan you pay the agent vendor.

Do I pay the agent vendor too?

Yes, when that path has a vendor plan. Those prices are listed above with links to the official pages, checked November 10, 2026. If a path is free software, you may still pay for a model, an API, or the machine that runs it.

What are future connections at $1?

After you start, each additional wire is $1. The path’s vendor plan, if it has one, stays a separate bill.
